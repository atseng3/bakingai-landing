FAQ

Things people ask.

If you don't see your question, drop it in the waitlist form below.

Can I really keep re-rolling the images?+
Yes — and most people do. Every re-roll is a fresh take on the same recipe: different angle, different styling, different mood. Each re-roll uses 1 credit; your monthly plan refills automatically, and top-ups never expire.
How do credits work?+
1 credit = 1 image generation. Generating a full recipe with all six formats uses 6 credits. Each re-roll of a single format uses 1. Hobbyist gets 50 credits a month, Creator gets 100. Credits reset every billing cycle. Top-up credits never expire — buy them any time inside the app.
Are the recipe photos real?+
No — they're AI-generated to match your recipe. Think of them as a target to bake toward, not a guarantee. We're clear about this in the app.
Are the recipes actually tested?+
Recipes are AI-generated using a model trained on tested baking ratios. They're reliable for standard bakes — treat exotic combinations as a starting point. Your taste test is the final word.
Can I use the images commercially?+
Personal use is included with every subscription. For commercial use — food blogs, bakery menus, packaging — reach out to hello@bakingai.co and we'll sort out a licensing arrangement that works for you.
What kitchens does Baking AI adapt to?+
You set a profile once — tools you own, oven type, units, dietary needs, skill level — and every recipe is generated to fit it.
Is it on Android or web?+
Not yet — iOS only at launch. Android is on the roadmap. Drop your email below to be notified.
